# Characters: visual and personality continuity

All names PROVISIONAL unless the story bible says otherwise. Visual specs are binding for artwork once a reference sheet is approved.

## Daisuke "Dai" Hayama (葉山 大輔), 17

- **Look:** 172 cm, lean and wiry, slight slouch, hands in pockets. Messy black hair, fringe falls over the eyes, a bit long at the back. Narrow, sharp, guarded eyes. **Small scar through the left eyebrow** (from the facility years), usually hidden by his fringe; draw it only in large close-ups. Approved reference: `artwork/character-sheets/dai.png`. Thin face.
- **Default outfit (pilot):** faded plain black T-shirt, worn straight-leg blue jeans, beat-up white canvas low-top sneakers. School: standard black gakuran, collar open, worn carelessly.
- **Personality:** quiet, prickly, quick temper he's learned to sit on (mostly). Proud. Hungry to be *seen*. Secretly tender with Sota. Doesn't explain himself, which makes every fight at home worse.

## Michiko Hayama, 41 (mother)

- **Look:** small, tired, older than her age. Black hair with some grey, pulled into a low loose bun with strands falling out. Fine lines around the eyes. Light-blue factory smock with a name tag, or a cardigan at home.
- **Personality:** practical, worn down, loves fiercely and shows it as worry and anger. Not a villain: she's the one keeping everyone fed.

## Aya Hayama, 20 (older sister)

- **Look:** chin-length black bob, the same sharp eyes as Dai. At home: grey tank top, cardigan, shorts.
- **Personality:** blunt, dry, exhausted. Gives half her paycheck to the household and resents that Dai doesn't. Under it, she worries about him most.

## Sota Hayama, 11 (younger brother)

- **Look:** small and thin for his age, round glasses, bowl-cut hair. Blue inhaler always near him.
- **Personality:** sweet, idolizes Dai. Asthma: his medicine is a recurring household cost.

## Nobuyuki "Nobu" Kawamata, 17 (classmate)

- **Look:** about 165 cm, stocky and soft, round full face with chubby cheeks, very short buzz-cut black hair (whole forehead visible), thick straight eyebrows, small bright eyes, a big mouth that is usually talking. No glasses. Summer uniform: white short-sleeved shirt untucked and a bit tight over the belly, black trousers, indoor shoes with the heels trodden flat, a worn canvas shoulder bag. Approved reference: `artwork/character-sheets/nobu.png` (2026-11-15). Designed to look nothing like Dai.
- **From the novel (Vol. 1 Ch. 1):** family ramen shop by the station (smells of soy sauce and pork bones), *Street Tune* reader since 13, a disposable camera in his bag, fast talker in horsepower figures, "Hayama-kun". "Call me Nobu."
- **Personality:** eager, over-explains, kind, tactless, apologizes fast and means it.

## Hotta-sensei (homeroom and English teacher)

- **Look (no sheet, minor):** thin, tired, late 40s, towel round his neck, a cram-school paper fan with a cartoon owl. Short-sleeved shirt, loosened tie.

## Goto, mid-20s

- **Look:** bleached, spiky, short hair, thin eyebrows, a cigarette nearly always. Shiny dark tracksuit jacket open over a white tank top, gold chain.
- **Car:** white Nissan Silvia S13 (see cars.md).
- **Personality:** loud, bitter about losing to the red Roadster. Not evil; a local racer with an ego.

## Akane Natsume (夏目 茜), 21 (driver of the red Roadster)

- **Not shown in the pilot** except a gloved hand and a silhouette.
- **Look (draft):** tall, long straight black hair in a low ponytail, calm unreadable eyes. Black leather driving gloves with the knuckles cut open.
- **Role:** the "Akai Zanzō." Later teaches Dai technique and mods, and believes in him.
