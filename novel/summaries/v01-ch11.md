# Previously: Volume 1, Chapter 11, "The Other Tarp"

Sunday 29 October 1995, 5:57 a.m. Dai pays Fumie her October 7,000 off the overpass grit; she writes *MINUS 7,000. 29 OCT.* At the lesson his feet know the heel-and-toe order but run a beat behind, then the nose pulls left under braking: a screw in Akane's front left tire. Sent for the trolley jack behind the tire wall, Dai finds the wind has loosened the grey tarp and lifts it. Under it is a black MR2, its right side folded round something upright, the door in to the middle of the seat, the windshield starred, a Momo wheel, a faded Shiomi Hachiman-gū charm. Akane stops her car at the end of the wall, looks at the wreck, not at him, and drives out without a word. Fumie: "Not my story."

That afternoon Kubo finds the gritty tensioner that ate the belt, fits the new pump, seals and belt, sets the marks and lets Dai turn the crank: now the engine pushes back. "That's what it didn't have." Then the front pads, on blocks: "Never under a jack." No battery yet: "Sunday." Fumie's wall comes to 13,000: "Customer." Tearing October off her calendar, she lets slip that Akane turns twenty-two on Friday.

Mom has sealed the Sanko form; Dai signs it and hands it in. Hotta: math 41, a pass. Payday is all his. Sota wants to come on Sunday and holds his lips shut until the inhaler.

On Thursday night Akane doesn't come. Dai changes a canopy tube at two with the road empty both ways. Friday, Culture Day, he walks to the overpass and finds her at the new guardrail. He buys two cans of corn soup. "Happy birthday." / "Tanabe-san." She almost laughs. "Two years ago a friend of mine went into that pillar. I was in front of him." He opens his mouth and shuts it. "Get your car running. I'll teach you to drive it. In the yard. You promised, kid." "I know."
