# Chapter 3: "Passenger Seat"

**Status:** draft. 28 pages, slow pacing.
**Reading order:** right-to-left.
**Story job:** a quiet week (first pay, a soft moment at home), then Thursday: Akane takes Dai for one lap meaning to scare him off the road. It backfires: he isn't scared, he's *watching her hands and feet*. She realizes he has a rare eye. Seed her past (a photo on her sun visor). End on Dai practicing shifting at dawn.
**Lettering:** empty balloons, lettered by `production/az letter` (first chapter with our own lettering).
**Proposal seed (creator can veto):** page 27 hints at Akane's past: a photo tucked in her sun visor of her with a young man next to a car; his face is out of focus. Ties to "that road eats people."
