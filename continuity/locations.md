# Locations (Shiomi City, PROVISIONAL)

- **Hayama apartment:** 2DK in an aging concrete danchi block on the city's east side. Tiny kitchen with a low table, tatami room behind sliding fusuma doors where Sota sleeps on a futon. Old CRT TV, rotary fan, laundry hanging indoors. Steel front door with a genkan step.
- **Danchi street:** narrow road, flickering streetlight, a drink vending machine that also sells canned beer (period-accurate in 1995).
- **Shiomi Bypass:** four-lane elevated-feeling road through the industrial edge of the city, long sweeping left-hand curve, orange sodium streetlights, concrete barriers. A **pedestrian overpass** crosses it right at the curve: Dai's spot.
- **Harbor district:** cranes, warehouses, container yards. Racers gather in a parking lot near Pier 3.
- **Harbor gas station:** small 24-hour full-service station on the harbor road, canopy lights, two pump islands, an older night attendant.
- **Shiomi Higashi High School:** Dai's school. Old classrooms, wooden desks, open windows, cicadas.
