# Previously: Volume 1, Chapter 12, "Ignition"

Saturday 4 November 1995. After half-day school Dai and Fumie prise the trunk open with a bar, take out the old battery, fit a used pair of taillights from Ishiguro (2,000) and the black top, and beat the rear plate nearly flat. The cat has to use the door now.

Sunday 5 November, two o'clock. Sota has his jacket on by ten. With Kubo, Fumie, Nobu and Sota watching, Kubo replaces a blown fuse for the lights, and when the new battery's black clamp touches, the stuck right headlight whirs down flat. He drains the March gasoline, pours in ten fresh liters, cranks for oil with the plugs out and fits new plugs. The first try is a click: the black clamp is loose. The second coughs, catches, shakes, ticks, and settles on one rough note. "Flowers on a dead tree," says Fumie. Dai drives Sota round the back lot at twenty. Sota laughs all the way along the Corollas, and the cough Dai waits for doesn't come. "Again." Fumie gives Dai the padlock and office keys: she'll lock up at four, and the cat eats at five. "Twice." Kubo: "Don't spend it all on gas."

All week Dai drives only the back lot. On Wednesday night he sits at the chained gate with the headlights on, then backs away. Akane doesn't come on Thursday or Friday.

Tuesday 14 November, 2:05 a.m. He slips out, unchains the gate and drives onto the harbor road at forty, shaking and laughing. At the red light before the rail crossing Goto's Silvia pulls up beside him. "Oi. Bridge kid. Nice car." The light changes; Goto goes. Dai's foot stays on the brake. He drives back and chains the gate. Eighty-seven days to eighteen.

## State at end
- About 3 a.m. Tuesday 14 November, at Tanabe's after the drive. School today, the shift tonight, payday Wednesday 15 Nov (Mom's half resumes).
- Dai has 49,101 yen: 46,000 in the dictionary (Fumie's November 7,000 among it, due 30 Nov), 3,101 on him. License: 230,899 to go. Fumie's wall still reads 13,000.
- The car runs: new battery, plugs and fuse, ten liters of fresh gasoline (needle a finger above E before the drive), taillights, the black top, a creased plate, a wired-down trunk lid, both headlights working. First start Sun 5 Nov; on the public road once.
- Dai holds Fumie's padlock and office keys.
- The promise was kept through the week and broken on Tue 14 Nov. Only Goto saw. Akane doesn't know the car runs.
- Kubo, Fumie, Nobu and Sota saw the start and the ride. Mom thinks Dai took Sota to look at the ships. Aya knows nothing.

## New PROVISIONAL facts
- Ishiguro's taillights 2,000 (off the burned white Roadster); the left sits a finger proud; one is scratched.
- Kubo's receipt: battery 6,800, four plugs 1,600, tax 252 (8,652) plus regular 10.0 L 1,150, 9,802 in all.
- The cause of the half-up headlight: a blown fuse for the pop-ups; Dai's guess that the man in March switched his lights off to save the battery.
- Fumie's keys on a wire loop with a tire-company tag; she locks up at four from Mon 6 Nov; the gate chained twice; the cat's half tin at five.
- Fumie's saying: "Flowers on a dead tree"; her mother said it of old men who married again.
- The car now lives in the back lot by the forklift corner; its old place is an oblong of pale dead grass.
- Departures from OUTLINE: Fumie gives the keys so she can lock up early and the cat still eats, not only to feed it; Akane also misses Friday 10 Nov.
