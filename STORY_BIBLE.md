# AKAI ZANZŌ (赤い残像, "Red Afterimage"): Story Bible

**Version:** 0.2 | **Status:** Pilot chapter in production

> Labels: **CANON** = confirmed by the creator. **PROVISIONAL** = chosen by Claude so the pilot could be made; the creator can replace it at any time, and it becomes canon only when the creator approves. **PROPOSAL** = discussion idea, not used yet. Agents must never quietly promote PROVISIONAL or PROPOSAL items to canon.

## 1. Core pitch [CANON]

A character-driven, slice-of-life street-racing manga set in Japan during the golden era of Japanese street racing. The protagonist is 17 around **1995**. He comes from a difficult, poor upbringing and becomes obsessed with cars after encountering a pristine red Mazda MX-5 NA (Japanese-market Eunos Roadster). He secretly learns to drive, enters the underground racing scene, and seeks respect and recognition while his obsession strains his finances and family relationships. The races are intense, but the emotional, everyday life is the story's center.

**Book One is solely about underground street racing.** A separate, later book covers his future as a famous, legitimate professional racer. Book One must stand on its own.

**Tone [CANON]:** gritty. City street racing means crashes have real consequences: injuries, wrecked cars, arrests. Not lighthearted or easygoing.

**Working title [CANON]:** *Akai Zanzō* (from the repo name). In-world, "Akai Zanzō" is the street name for the red Roadster [PROVISIONAL].

## 2. Protagonist [CANON]

- Male, about 17 in 1995; working-class, poor.
- Lives in a medium-sized Japanese city.
- Raised by a **single mother with three children**. He is the **middle child**.
- Bullied at school; difficult childhood.
- Lived in an institution for children with serious behavioral/social difficulties from **ages 10 to 14** (Japanese context: research 児童自立支援施設 before depicting it in detail).
- Wants to prove that someone with his background can become exceptional, someone thousands admire.
- Somewhat self-insert in spirit, but written as a coherent, flawed character who is sometimes wrong in ways the story does not excuse.

**Name [PROVISIONAL]:** Daisuke "Dai" Hayama (葉山 大輔).

## 3. Inciting incident [CANON]

At 17, on a night walk while drinking (**alcohol, replacing cannabis**; underage, which is its own small rebellion), he sees an immaculate **red NA Eunos Roadster** speed past. The engine note hits him emotionally and sparks his obsession.

## 4. The red Roadster and its driver [CANON]

- For **a few weeks** after the sighting he chases rumors of a super-fast pristine Miata, trying to find out who drives it.
- The driver turns out to be a **woman**. He falls in love with her.
- She becomes his **first ally in the scene**: she teaches him technique and which mods matter, and she believes in him.

**PROVISIONAL details:** Name Akane Natsume (夏目 茜), 21. Because Dai is 17 at the start, nothing romantic happens between them until he is 18; at first she treats him as a kid with raw talent, and his feelings are a one-sided crush. The romance should be slow, earned, and complicated (her own past in the scene, the age gap, his immaturity), which keeps it from feeling like a cliché.

**Undecided:** Whether the car he later owns is hers; exactly how he gets his first car.

## 5. Early timeline [CANON]

- **Circa 1995, age 17:** The sighting triggers his passion.
- **Last three months before 18:** Secretly practices **illegal, unlicensed** driving. A few existing street racers notice but don't see him as a threat.
- **Turns 18:** Gets his license quickly, then keeps climbing in underground racing.
- **Afterward:** Builds local recognition gradually.

**Research note:** In 1990s Japan a license usually meant driving school (roughly ¥250,000-300,000). How a poor kid pays for it is a strong story hook (possible first reason to bet on himself) [PROPOSAL].

## 6. Racing and money [CANON]

- **Illegal city street racing** (urban roads, bypasses, harbor and industrial districts), not sanctioned motorsport and not mountain touge.
- He earns money through **bets placed on himself**. His family **does not know**.
- Poverty and recurring costs matter; racing is neither a reliable income nor a free hobby.
- Rivals initially underestimate him; reputation is earned, never instant.

## 7. Family conflict and thematic center [CANON]

His mother wants him to face reality, get a job, and build a stable life. Representative sentiment:

> "You're refusing to accept reality. You need a job. You can't just race your way to a paycheck."

**He sees driving as his way out; his family sees it as another danger that could destroy his future.** Both perspectives are taken seriously.

## 8. Tone and format [CANON]

- Character-driven slice of life: family, school, job, friendship, car culture. Racing is high-intensity punctuation.
- Gritty, but celebrates the tactile appeal of cars and enthusiast culture.
- Mid-1990s Japan is essential. No smartphones, internet culture, or anachronistic vehicles/tech.
- **Format:** personal reading project. Black-and-white manga pages, **English lettering**, read **right-to-left** like scanlations on MangaDex.
- Development starts with a **pilot chapter** to test story and art consistency.

## 9. Two-book boundary [CANON]

**Book I:** street racing, local reputation, youthful ambition, money, family, underground culture.
**Book II (separate):** legitimate professional racing. Do not move pro-racing arcs into Book I.

## 10. Supporting cast and setting [PROVISIONAL]

See `continuity/characters.md`, `continuity/cars.md`, `continuity/locations.md`.

- **City:** Shiomi (潮見市), a fictional mid-sized Pacific-coast port and factory city (~400,000 people) with a harbor loop, a four-lane bypass, warehouse districts, and old danchi housing blocks.
- **Mother:** Michiko Hayama, 41, bento factory by day, supermarket shelves at night.
- **Older sister:** Aya Hayama, 20, supermarket cashier, gives half her pay to the household.
- **Younger brother:** Sota Hayama, 11, asthma (medicine is a recurring cost).
- **Goto:** 20s street racer in a white Nissan Silvia S13; first person to tell Dai about the "Akai Zanzō."

## 11. Discussion ideas [PROPOSALS, NOT CANON]

- **Rival:** a wealthy, well-supported, genuinely decent racer whose advantages challenge Dai's assumptions.
- **Emotional question:** "If nobody watched you race, would you still drive?"
- **Family crisis:** Mother finds unexplained cash, suspects crime, learns about the racing, and says she mostly wants him to come home alive.
- **Moral failure:** He picks a car upgrade over Sota's urgent medical expense, then regrets it.
- **End of Book I:** hard-won but bittersweet reputation, unresolved costs, still satisfying.
- **Book II motif:** he stays attached to his early Miata despite fame.

## 13. Production workflow

- Claude writes scripts and art direction, and commissions artwork from **GPT-6.1 Terra** (Codex image generation).
- Reference sheets first (`artwork/character-sheets`, `artwork/car-sheets`), then pages, each page prompt carrying the relevant reference images.
- Art direction lives in `prompts/art-direction.md`; provenance per asset in `artwork/PROVENANCE.md`.
- Cars, wheels, headlight state (pop-ups up/down), steering side (**right-hand drive**), damage, clothing and locations must stay consistent across pages.
