# Previously: manga chapter 2, "Thursday"

The next morning Sota, 11, has a coughing fit, and Dai kneels beside him with the blue inhaler: "Slow. Breathe with me." Sota finds the Roadster sketch, and Dai tells him about the red car that "ate the corner." Sota is thrilled and promises not to tell Mom that Dai came home late. Michiko leaves Sota's lunch money on the shoe cabinet without looking at Dai, and the door closes on him quietly.

Dai works out that hanging around a gas station every night would get him thrown out, unless he had a reason to be there. At the harbor station by day he asks the old attendant, Kubo, for a job. Kubo remembers him as "the kid who was screaming at my customer" and asks why here. "I need the money." Kubo: 700 yen an hour, nights, "be late once, you're gone." Dai bows for the first time in the story.

He learns the work: waving trucks in, windshields, checking oil. When an old sedan squeals, Kubo hears a dying belt and Dai hears something more, a tick on the left side. Kubo: "...Huh."

Thursday comes slowly. Night shift, rain, Kubo goes to nap, and at last, through the rain, the engine note and the pop-ups. The driver is revealed: a young woman, calm and unimpressed. "Regular. Fill it up." Dai asks if she is the Akai Zanzō ("Is that what they call it now"), tells her he saw her beat the Silvia, admits he can't drive, and says he wants to drive like that. She tells him, "That road eats people. Stay off it." Then she asks his name and gives hers: "Natsume."

At dawn Dai meets his mother on the stairs, in his station jacket. "I got a job."

## State at end
- Dai works nights at the harbor station under Kubo, 700 yen an hour.
- He knows the driver only as "Natsume" and that she comes on Thursdays.
- Michiko, surprised, begins to hope; she doesn't know why he took the job.
- Sota shares Dai's secrets; Kubo has noticed Dai's ear for engines.
