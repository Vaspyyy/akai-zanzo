## Checks
- **Two Roadsters.** The red one appears only on p4 (front three-quarter of its right flank at the pump, pop-ups down) and p5 (rear three-quarter driving away, pop-ups up), clean and undamaged. Every other Roadster is the wreck, and its marks hold throughout: grey faded paint chalky on top, the white left front fender, the primer hood, the right pop-up stuck half up, the crushed rear, the torn roof over the passenger seat. The fish-truck memory (p13) shows it before the crash, still in one piece.
- **Wreck shots, no repeated pose:** p9 roof over the weeds, rear-fender crop, then an elevated front three-quarter over Dai's shoulder; p10 door close-ups and a low head-on; p11 low rear three-quarter from behind-right and a roof from above; p12 the driver's side with the front to the RIGHT (right-hand drive); p13 trunk close-up and the high memory shot from a truck cab; p14 white fender close-up and a rear three-quarter from behind-left; p17 windshield frame and the empty seat; p30 interior only.
- **Fumie** matches her sheet on every page: towel, cigarette, left eye half shut, rolled sleeves, steel-toed boots, black left thumbnail. She sits on the forklift on p6-8 and is on foot from p13. She wears leather gloves on Sunday.
- **Dai's left hand** is bandaged from p28 to the end. He wears white cotton gloves on Sunday until the cut. His clothes: summer uniform p1-2, the grey station jacket p2-19, a black T-shirt at home, the gym shirt on Sunday.
- **Light:** night at the station; early sun on the Friday walk; hazy morning in the yard; daylight, then evening at home; Sunday from bright midday to a low yellow sun on p30.
- **The grey tarp** (p29) is only a roped grey canvas shape with tires on the corners. Nothing under it is shown.
- No brand logos. The only readable sign is the fence's TANABE AUTO DISMANTLING (p6), faded TANABE and dark DISMANTLING as in the novel.
