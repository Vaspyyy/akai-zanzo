# Chapter 7: "Forty-One"

**Split:** second half of Volume 1 Chapter 3 ("Pier 3"), from the Silvia's launch to the end of the novel chapter. Chapter 6 ended on the starter's flashlight coming down.
**Status:** draft. 30 pages, right-to-left. **Source:** `novel/volume-01/chapter-03.md` (from "He held it there. I counted two.").
**Lettering:** empty balloons, English scanlation lettering. One speaker per panel. Dry September night, 1995 (about 1:30 to 4:30 a.m., Sunday 10 September), moon up. No red Roadster anywhere. All cars are Japanese models sold by September 1995, right-hand drive.

**Race geography (STYLE §6), fixed for every page:**
- The Loop: Pier 3 lot → the warehouse straight (1 km, dead flat, warehouse walls on the LEFT, container yard and water on the RIGHT) → rail crossing (single freight track with a hump, one light on a pole) → road bends LEFT behind the grain silos → S-bend between the silos → on-ramp up to the elevated bypass → straight → the long left-hander under Dai's pedestrian overpass → Higashi exit → down onto the far end of the harbor road → back past the lot (finish = start line, crossed in the same direction).
- Start grid as in Ch6 p30: Silvia LEFT lane (warehouse side), Chaser RIGHT lane. The Chaser passes on the right.
- **Screen direction:** in every lateral or three-quarter race shot the cars travel toward the LEFT of the panel (noses left), so we see their LEFT flanks and the drivers stay in the dark far side of the cabin. Views from the lot (Dai's eyes) look straight down the road at the cars' tails. Head-on shots only where the cars come at the camera in single file.
- Who leads, page by page: p1-2 Silvia by a length, gap shrinking; p3 Chaser alongside, then past, three lengths; p4 Chaser still three up at the crossing, hits the bump; p5-6 Chaser ahead, Silvia close; p7 Silvia takes the inside at the silos and leads; p9 Silvia two lengths up the on-ramp; p10 Chaser closes on the bypass straight; p11 Silvia through the curve, Chaser brakes mid-corner; p12 Silvia wins by two lengths.
- **Car shots, one camera each (no repeats):** p1 low rear three-quarter behind the Chaser's right rear corner; p2 elevated front three-quarter from ahead-left; p3 straight top-down; p4 low lateral at rail level (Chaser), straight rear small (Chaser wobble), high rear three-quarter from behind-right (Silvia alone); p7 high oblique from a silo top on the outside of the bend; p9 low from beneath the on-ramp looking up; p10 low head-on from the median; p11 high from behind on the overpass; p12 far head-on lights only, then low front three-quarter at the finish, then rear three-quarter from behind-left braking; p19 high front view of the Silvia swinging round, lights off; p20 close tracking crop along the Chaser's left side; Crown: p21 eye-level front three-quarter of its left flank, high rear three-quarter from behind-right turning in; p22 distant lights from the doorway; p26 far, high, leaving.
