# Cars: technical continuity

For automotive manga, technical consistency is storytelling. Every page that shows a car must match its sheet.

## The red Roadster ("Akai Zanzō"), driven by Akane

- **Model:** 1990 Mazda **Eunos Roadster** (NA6CE, Japanese-market NA MX-5), 1.6 L, ~120 PS.
- **Color:** classic red (Classic Red), immaculate paint, no damage, no stickers, no badges added.
- **Drive side:** **right-hand drive** (Japanese market). Driver sits on the right.
- **Top:** black soft top, **up** in the pilot.
- **Headlights:** pop-up. State must be drawn deliberately: up when driving at night, down when parked.
- **Wheels:** silver 14-inch 7-spoke alloy wheels (matches artwork/car-sheets/red-roadster.png, approved reference), same in every panel. Slightly lowered, otherwise stock-looking body: no wing, no body kit.
- **Trim:** a base car. **No power steering** (heavy at a crawl, lighter as speed rises), **wind-up windows** (a handle, not a button) and **manual door mirrors** pushed by hand; the passenger mirror means leaning across and winding the passenger window down. The alloy wheels were fitted later, not by the factory (who fitted them is not stated).
- **Gearing (NA6, for engine notes):** in second, 20 km/h is about 1,500 rpm and 25 km/h about 1,900; below about 15 km/h second hammers. In first, 20 km/h is about 2,500 rpm. Idle sits just under 1,000.
- **Plate:** white Japanese plate; details not legible.
- **Sound:** high, clean, rising four-cylinder wail. SFX: "WAAAAAN".

## Goto's car

- **Model:** Nissan **Silvia S13** (fixed slim headlights, NOT pop-ups; do not confuse with the 180SX).
- **Color:** white, slightly dirty.
- **Mods:** big aftermarket rear wing, black multi-spoke wheels, loud exhaust.
- **Right-hand drive.**

## The black MR2 under Fumie's grey tarp (novel V1 ch11; manga ch15)

Only what Dai saw when he lifted the canvas (Sun 29 Oct 1995). Everything here is PROVISIONAL.

- **Model:** Toyota **MR2**, the first-generation (AW11) shape: low and wedge-shaped, mid-engine (a long flat deck behind the doors), a blunt nose with no grille, **pop-up headlights shut down flat**, an engine air vent behind the door (Dai saw only the right side). Model year not stated.
- **Color:** black, dull under a grey film of dust; deep black where the dust is wiped off.
- **Drive side:** right-hand drive.
- **Damage (right side, the driver's side):** the front right wheel pushed back into its arch and turned in, its tire flat and cracked grey; the fender behind the headlight creased twice; **the door folded into a deep U from sill to roof** round something upright not much wider than a person (an arm fits into it up to the elbow), the sill bent up off the ground, the roof kinked above it, the window gone (a fringe of glass cubes left in the bottom rubber); **inside, the door reaches the middle of the seat**; the vent behind the door pushed in with it.
- **Windshield:** in place but gone white: a star low on the right, in front of the wheel, with cracks running to every edge; clear only in the bottom far corner.
- **Steering wheel:** not the factory one: small, thick leather rim, three spokes, **MOMO** in white on the horn button.
- **Mirror charm:** a brocade traffic-safety charm on a cord, red on one side, faded to pink on the sun side; TRAFFIC SAFETY in gold thread down the front, SHIOMI HACHIMAN-GŪ on the back.
- **Where:** at the very back of Tanabe's, against the back fence at the end of the weed strip behind the tire wall; heavy grey canvas (every other cover in the yard is blue plastic). After Dai lifted it, Fumie added two ropes (over the roof and the nose, tied low to the fence posts) and a fifth tire on top.
