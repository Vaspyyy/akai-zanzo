# Previously: Volume 1, Chapter 10, "Side Bet"

Friday 27 October 1995, 6 a.m. Kubo asks what Dai saw behind the wheels at Tanabe's. Rusty discs, pads thinner than a 10-yen coin. "Pads. The fronts. After Sunday it'll roll. Then it'll want to stop." About 6,000; Dai will bring it Sunday. At school Hotta: "Tuesday, Hayama. Don't make me come to your house."

That evening Kazue Sasaki comes to the door with Ken's outgrown winter jacket for Sota. Mom refuses twice and takes it the third time. When the door shuts she bows to it. Inside the collar, white tape covers SASAKI, with nothing written on it yet.

Saturday afternoon Mom comes in from the factory and, standing in the genkan, asks: "Can you give me twenty thousand yen." The hospital, the new medicine, the rent on Tuesday. Dai gives her Wednesday's half four days early, 22,400, and another 10,000. She takes it all and writes in her notebook: *28 Oct. Daisuke. 32,400.* "Your rice is cold." The pharmacy receipt says 7,180 a month now. Dai has 11,297 and can pay Fumie's 7,000 or the pads, not both.

He tells Mom he's sleeping at Kawamata's and goes to Pier 3 alone. A black A80 Supra against the white Starlet boy from September. The favorite's right front tire is worn to the cords on the inside edge; the owner's hands won't keep still. Dai guesses at the backs, wipes his palm like the owner, and puts his last 10,000 on the Starlet: ten pays thirty, less Okabe's tenth of the winnings. The Supra spins at the silos and limps back on a torn rear. Okabe pays 28,000. "You've got an eye. Ever bet on a sure thing?... Yourself?"

Walking back with the warm notes, Dai remembers his father: pachinko balls running out of his trouser turn-ups in the genkan, the jam jar, two polite men at the door when Dai was nine. Between the silos he can't tell whether it was the tire, the man's foot or luck. He sleeps on the overpass and at dawn counts the money into hours at the pumps.

- Dai has 29,297 yen: 7,000 set aside for Fumie in his shirt pocket, 21,097 in his trouser pocket, 1,200 at home. Net win 18,000. He plans to pay Fumie and about 6,000 for the pads on Sunday (Ch11). Wednesday's pay is all his (Mom's half was advanced).
- Mom has 32,400 from him, written in her notebook, and thinks he slept at Kawamata's. Sota has Ken's jacket. Nobody knows about Pier 3 but Okabe.
