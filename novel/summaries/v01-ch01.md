# Previously: Volume 1, Chapter 1, "Practicing"

Friday 25 August 1995, dawn after the lap. Dai sits on his futon working an invisible gear lever with his left hand and heel-and-toeing on air. Sota wakes, guesses it's "for the red car", and learns the gear pattern ("Other hand"). Dai promises him the feet "next week", sleeps two hours, and goes to the last day of summer remedial English.

Hotta-sensei, his tired homeroom teacher, runs the re-test. Nobuyuki Kawamata, a ramen-shop son who has read every issue of *Street Tune* since he was thirteen, lends Dai a pen, spots his pedal drawing and won't stop talking. When Dai says the Akai Zanzō beat a white S13 and that he rode in it last night, Nobu laughs: "I didn't know you were funny." Dai won't say who drives it. Both pass. Nobu asks about "reform school" ("A place."), mentions that his cousin's license cost 280,000 yen, and says "Call me Nobu."

At a bookstore Dai reads a driving book's heel-and-toe photos until the owner dusts him out with a feather duster. On the back of an onigiri receipt he does the math: twelve half-pay paydays, ending 7 February 1996, two days before he turns 18. He crosses out the version where he keeps all his pay.

At dinner Mom tells him exactly where his first pay went, his foot kicks Aya under the table, and Mom notices his open shoe. On Sunday night Kubo explains why you blip on a downshift ("So it lands on its feet"); Dai notices the old burn scar and black knuckles on Kubo's hand. "Thursdays. Like burnable garbage."

Thursday 31 August: she doesn't come. Kubo: "She's not a train." At dawn on the overpass Dai sees, for the first time, a newer section of guardrail and an old scrape at the foot of the stairs, scrubbed pale.

## State at end
- Friday 1 September, about 6:45 a.m.; second term starts at 8:30.
- Dai has 18,677 yen (17,600 in his English dictionary). Next pay Wed 6 Sep: 44,800.
- Akane missed a Thursday for the first time Kubo knows of. Dai fears "one lap" meant one.
- Nobu knows Dai's claims and believes none of them; Dai still has his pen.
- Sota knows Dai practices "for the red car"; nobody else in the family knows anything.
- Dai has noticed Kubo's mechanic's hands and the crash marks under the overpass, and connects neither.

- Mom's schedule: up at 4:30, factory 5:30 to 2:00, supermarket from about 8:00 p.m. to midnight. She spent his 19,600: pharmacy 4,820, Sota's school things 2,150, rent 12,630.
