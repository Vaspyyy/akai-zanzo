# Previously: Volume 1, Chapter 5, "Interview"

Sunday night, 17 September 1995. Kubo sees the bandage: "Tanabe's?" / "Window motor. Corolla." He works out the Sundays.

Monday evening Aya cuts his hair at the kitchen table with professional scissors nobody knew she had, in total silence. She combs his bangs up, sees the pale scar through his left eyebrow, lets the bangs fall, then cuts them off anyway: "They want eyebrows." He looks like somebody's son. "At the place by the station, that's three thousand yen."

Tuesday, 5 a.m.: Mom irons his summer shirt, mentions next Monday's overnight stocktaking, sees the bandage ("The sign out by the road") and gives him a clean roll. The shirt is still warm. At Sanko Brake he is good at the Kraepelin adding test and keeps his lips shut. In the interview Sugimoto of Personnel reads question 10 off the sheet, his eyes on the junior-high line. Dai presses his fist into his knee until the cut bleeds, and says it himself: "From ten to fourteen I lived in a facility, and since then I've gone to school like everybody else." An old line worker: "You learn the sound it makes when something's wrong. Before anything breaks." Dai has no idea if he passed.

Kubo: "Did you shout at anybody?" Payday. On Friday morning Natsume sees the bandage: "Keep that dry."

Monday night, 25 September, Mom is at the stocktaking. At 12:50 a.m. Sota wakes wheezing. Two puffs; Dai waits the twenty minutes the clinic paper says, though Sota can hardly talk. Two more. The whistle goes quiet, his lips go grey-blue, and Aya freezes. Dai sends her down to the road and dials 119 on the rotary phone. Sota, between breaths: "Ambulance... costs..." "It's free." In the ambulance, under the oxygen mask, Dai tells him about the car: "You're first. Don't tell Mom." In the ER the crewman says he did the times right, but next time don't wait. The curtain opens: "Hayama Sota's family?" It's Natsume.
