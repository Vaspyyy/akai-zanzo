# Previously: Volume 1, Chapter 9, "Type R"

Thursday 19 October 1995. Mom leaves two onigiri on the shoe cabinet with no note. At 2 a.m. Dai hides the second one behind his back at the pump. Natsume: "Eat it." He eats it at her window. "I don't eat breakfast." / "None?" / "Coffee."

Saturday, Sota's Mini 4WD race at Fujita's hobby shop. Sota pays his own 100 yen in 10-yen coins from a film canister. Ken Sasaki, Mai's big brother from the ward, has a tackle box full of parts: "Stickers aren't parts." Sota's car flies off after the bridge every time and loses its heat. Dai sees why: it lands nose-up. He tapes two 10-yen coins under its nose, and it goes round and round on its own. "It can't steer, Nii-chan. It just goes till the wall tells it." They laugh. Kazue Sasaki ("I'm always asleep in that chair") writes her number on the back of a receipt: "For nights. My husband drives a taxi. Give it to your mother. Not to Sota." Mom reads it and puts it in the phone book under the phone without a word.

Sunday's lesson starts heel-and-toe. "Those are too thick," Natsume says of his shoes. He gets one clean downshift. Fumie: "Six. One more Sunday. Then you're a customer." That night Dai pays Kubo 27,810 for the parts. "The labor." / "What labor?" / "Next Sunday. After two."

Monday Nobu helps carry a used top from Ishiguro's body shop, an hour on foot past the Sanko gate. At the yard Nobu goes quiet, wants one photo, is refused, and swears on his twenty-six exposures. His father serves passing gyoza, and Nobu teaches Dai the parabola for the midterms. Thursday night Aya giggles in the payphone and shoos him away. At 2:05 Natsume has a paperback: "That cap makes you look twelve." At 4 a.m. a brand-new white Integra Type R, being run in, asks whether a red Roadster fills up here. "No." He reads the name tag. "You're a bad liar, Hayama-kun."

## State at end
- About 4 a.m. Friday 27 October, under the canopy at Kubo's. Midterms are over (results not back). Lesson, last work Sunday and Kubo's belt job on Sunday 29 October; 7,000 to Fumie and the Sanko form due Tuesday 31 October; payday Wednesday 1 November.
- Dai has 43,697 yen (43,200 in the dictionary, 497 in pocket); 236,303 to the license. Six of seven Sundays worked.
- The car: parts bought and waiting at Kubo's, used top bought and lying beside it; still doesn't run.
- Nobu knows the yard and the car and has sworn to keep it secret. Mom has Kazue Sasaki's number. Natsume doesn't know about the belt or the Type R driver.
