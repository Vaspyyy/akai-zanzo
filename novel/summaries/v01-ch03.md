# Previously: Volume 1, Chapter 3, "Pier 3"

Saturday 9 September 1995, 4 a.m., end of the Friday shift. Nobu has dared Dai with a *Street Tune* letter about Saturday nights at the harbor. Kubo: "Pier 3. The big lot at the end. After one." He won't say if he's been.

Saturday 23:20, harvest moon. Dai tells Aya he's studying at Kawamata's; she owes him the haircut and will tell Mom he's staying over. Sota asks him to look at the moon for him, properly. Nobu, in kitchen sandals, has told his father he's studying at Dai's: "Then nobody's anywhere."

They walk the back road to the silos, over the rail crossing and down the warehouse straight. The lot fills: Eurobeat, fifty-three people, Okabe with a cash box and a school notebook. Tetsu calls the white S13's driver "Goto-san". Goto knows "the bridge kid": "Still chasing afterimages?" Dai stands on Nobu's foot to keep him quiet.

Goto runs a grey Chaser Tourer V for 50,000 each. The Chaser is favorite until its driver asks the way after the silos and Goto finds mold hairs on its new tires; the odds flip. Dai hears the Chaser lift at the silos before the radio says so. Goto wins by two lengths: 90,000 back, 40,000 up, the exact sum in Dai's dictionary. Okabe to a boy with 15,000: "You can always bet on yourself."

"Patrol. On-ramp." The lot empties in twenty seconds. Dai and Nobu hide in a warehouse doorway. An older officer pockets a betting chit and looks at their dark doorway, and at Nobu's lone sandal, for forty-one seconds, then leaves. Dai looks at the moon for Sota. Walking home, Dai gives Nobu his socks, and Nobu starts to believe him. At the driving-school fence: forty thousand yen for four minutes, and no car.
