# Previously: Volume 1, Chapter 8, "Timing Belt"

Sunday 8 October 1995, 3:10 p.m. After a second lesson (four stalls, not eleven), Kubo rides into Tanabe's on a green Super Cub with a toolbox older than Dai. Fumie: "Kubo. Still alive." Dai cranks the dead Roadster; the starter spins fast and light, the beat all wrong. "Half of it isn't pushing back." The cam doesn't move when Kubo turns the crank. Under the upper cover: a snapped timing belt, a hand's length of teeth stripped. Kubo drops one rubber tooth into Dai's palm. "Something's been eating that belt. Probably the tensioner." The 1.6 doesn't wreck its valves; Dai turns the engine by hand and nothing jams. Fumie: "They still bring me cars with a belt." "I had a garage. My own. Twelve years." / "The bubble." He'll get the parts.

At dinner Aya smells gasoline on Dai and says nothing. Next day a 168-yen bar of grit soap from her supermarket sits by the sink. She never looks at it.

The next Sunday Fumie drives Dai and his wheels to Okabe Tire by the canal. Okabe knows him: "The kid who watches money." He teaches him to read used tires and sells him four for 11,000, on a receipt made out to TANABE. "Business is business." The car stands on its own wheels.

Monday Dai sleeps through class. Hotta warns him: midterms on the 24th; no graduation, no job, and the next boys pay for it. Wednesday is payday, and the Sanko letter is in the box. Mom waits up with it, reads it twice, cries at the stove without a sound, and pins it to the fridge. Aya: "Now you can pay me my three thousand yen." At 4 a.m. Dai stands in front of the letter with the rubber tooth in his hand.

## State at end
- About 4 a.m. Thursday 19 October, the kitchen. Thursday shift tonight; Kubo's parts Saturday 21 October, price unknown; lesson Sunday 22 October; midterms from Tuesday 24 October.
- Dai has 76,687 yen (76,200 in the dictionary, 487 in pocket); 203,313 to the license. Owes Fumie 7,000 by 31 October; five of seven Sundays worked. 22,400 to Mom on each payday.
- The car: snapped timing belt, non-interference engine, tensioner suspected; on four used tires.
- Sanko offers Production at the Shiomi plant from 1 April 1996, if he graduates and passes a health check; the form goes back through the school by 31 October.
- Kubo knows about the car. Natsume doesn't know about the belt.
