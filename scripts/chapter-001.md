# Chapter 1: "Red Afterimage" (pilot)

**Status:** draft for creator review. 20 pages (pages 8-9 are one double-page spread, so 19 images).
**Reading order:** right-to-left. "P1 (top-right)" = first panel.
**Story job of this chapter:** show who Dai is and what he lacks (home, money, being seen), hit him with the red Roadster, and end with a hook: he's found a trail to the driver.

---

## Page 1: Shiomi at night

- **P1 (top, wide, 2/3 of page):** Establishing shot of Shiomi City at night, summer 1995. Harbor cranes and factory smokestacks against the sky, lit windows of old concrete danchi apartment blocks in the foreground. 
  - CAPTION: "SHIOMI CITY. AUGUST, 1995."
- **P2 (bottom, wide):** Closer: one lit window on the 4th floor of a danchi block, laundry hanging on the balcony.
  - SFX (small, in the air): "MEEN MEEN MEEN" (cicadas)

## Page 2: The apartment

- **P1 (top, wide):** Cramped kitchen with a low table. MICHIKO (factory smock) eats leftover rice late at night. AYA counts coins into a household envelope. Behind half-open sliding doors, SOTA sleeps on a futon, blue inhaler next to his pillow. DAI at the steel front door, crouched in the genkan, pulling on his white sneakers.
  - MICHIKO: "WHERE ARE YOU GOING?"
  - DAI: "OUT."
- **P2 (middle-right):** Close-up of Michiko, tired eyes, chopsticks down.
  - MICHIKO: "THE CONVENIENCE STORE CALLED. YOU DIDN'T SHOW UP FOR YOUR SHIFT. AGAIN."
- **P3 (middle-left):** Dai, not turning around, tying a lace.
  - DAI: "THE MANAGER'S AN IDIOT."
- **P4 (bottom):** Aya, not looking up from the coins.
  - AYA: "THE IDIOT PAYS 650 YEN AN HOUR."

## Page 3: Reality

- **P1 (top-right):** Michiko stands up.
  - MICHIKO: "DAISUKE. LOOK AT ME."
- **P2 (top-left):** Michiko, hand pressed on the table, voice cracking.
  - MICHIKO: "SOTA'S MEDICINE WENT UP AGAIN. AYA GIVES HALF HER PAY. I WORK TWO JOBS."
- **P3 (middle, wide):** Michiko and Dai facing each other across the tiny room.
  - MICHIKO: "YOU'RE SEVENTEEN. YOU NEED TO FACE REALITY. GET A JOB. KEEP IT."
- **P4 (bottom-right):** Dai's face half in shadow, eyes hidden by his fringe.
  - DAI: "...REALITY, HUH."
- **P5 (bottom-left):** The steel door slammed shut, Aya and Michiko left inside.
  - SFX: "BAM!"
  - AYA: "MOM..."
  - MICHIKO (small balloon): "LET HIM GO."

## Page 4: Vending machine

- **P1 (top, wide):** Dai walks down the narrow danchi street, hands in pockets, under a flickering streetlight.
- **P2 (middle-right):** A glowing drink vending machine with canned beer in its display. Dai's hand pushes a button.
  - SFX: "GACHON"
- **P3 (middle-left):** Close-up: his finger cracks open the beer can tab.
  - SFX: "PSSHT"
  - CAPTION: "REALITY."
- **P4 (bottom, wide):** Dai, small in frame, walking along an empty street with the can.
  - CAPTION: "REALITY IS A GUY NOBODY REMEMBERS."

## Page 5: Nobody

- **P1 (top-right):** Memory, softer screentone, panel border wavy: a school desk carved with "GO BACK TO YOUR CAGE", classmates sneering in the background.
  - CAPTION: "THE KID FROM THE FACILITY."
- **P2 (top-left):** Memory: a long institutional corridor, a 10-year-old Dai (same scar-less face, same hair) sitting alone on a bench.
  - CAPTION: "FOUR YEARS IN THE PLACE THEY SEND KIDS NOBODY CAN HANDLE."
- **P3 (bottom, large):** Present. Dai leans on the railing of a pedestrian overpass above an empty four-lane road, can in hand, looking down.
  - CAPTION: "SEVENTEEN YEARS, AND NOBODY HAS EVER LOOKED AT ME AND THOUGHT..."
  - CAPTION: "...'WOW.'"

## Page 6: 2:14 AM

- **P1 (top, wide):** Quiet wide shot: the overpass, the empty Shiomi Bypass curving away under orange sodium lights (dark tones), concrete barriers.
  - CAPTION: "2:14 AM."
- **P2 (middle):** Close-up of Dai. He lifts his head. Something in the distance.
  - SFX (small, faint): "...nnnNNN"
- **P3 (bottom, wide):** Far down the road in the darkness, two pop-up headlights flip open.
  - SFX: "KA-CHAK"

## Page 7: Coming in hot

- **P1 (top, wide):** Huge SFX across the panel: "WAAAAAAAN". Dai grips the railing, eyes wide.
- **P2 (bottom, large):** Low dramatic angle on the road: the red Eunos Roadster bursts out of the darkness, headlights up, speed lines. Close behind it, Goto's white Silvia S13 with its big wing, tailgating.

## Pages 8-9: DOUBLE SPREAD (one landscape image)

- **Single panel:** The long left-hand curve beneath the overpass. The red Roadster dives to the inside line, perfectly balanced, tires at the limit. The Silvia runs wide toward the outer barrier, losing ground. Dai is a small silhouette on the overpass above, leaning over the rail. Speed lines, motion blur.
  - SFX: "KYAAAAAA" (tires)
  - SFX: "WAAAAAN" (engine)
  - No dialogue.

## Page 10: Red

- **P1 (top, wide):** The Roadster passes directly under the overpass; Dai spins to follow it to the other side.
- **P2 (middle):** Extreme close-up of Dai's eye with two red taillights reflected in it.
- **P3 (bottom, wide):** The taillights streak away into the night, leaving long light trails: an afterimage.
  - CAPTION: "RED."

## Page 11: What was that

- **P1 (top-right):** Far down the road, the Silvia pulls onto the shoulder, hazard lights blinking.
- **P2 (top-left):** The beer can falls from Dai's hand and rolls on the overpass floor.
  - SFX: "KARAN... KARAN..."
- **P3 (middle):** Dai's hand on the railing, trembling.
- **P4 (bottom-right):** Dai, stunned.
  - DAI: "...WHAT... WAS THAT?"
- **P5 (bottom-left):** Close-up on his chest, heartbeat.
  - SFX: "BA-DUMP"

## Page 12: Goto

- **P1 (top-right):** Dai runs down the overpass stairs.
- **P2 (top-left):** Dai jogs along the bypass sidewalk toward the parked white Silvia.
- **P3 (middle, wide):** GOTO leans on his Silvia, lighting a cigarette, annoyed. He notices Dai.
  - GOTO: "OI, KID. YOU SEE WHERE IT WENT?"
- **P4 (bottom-right):** Dai, out of breath.
  - DAI: "THAT RED CAR... WHO DRIVES IT?"
- **P5 (bottom-left):** Goto laughs bitterly, smoke rising.
  - GOTO: "NOBODY KNOWS."

## Page 13: Akai Zanzō

- **P1 (top, wide):** Goto, looking down the empty road.
  - GOTO: "GUYS AROUND THE HARBOR CALL IT THE 'AKAI ZANZŌ.'"
  - GOTO: "THE RED AFTERIMAGE."
- **P2 (middle-right):** Goto's cynical face close-up.
  - GOTO: "YOU SEE IT. YOU CHASE IT. AND ALL YOU EVER CATCH IS THE AFTERIMAGE."
- **P3 (middle-left):** Goto flicks his cigarette away and opens his car door.
  - GOTO: "SOME RICH GUY'S TOY. FORGET IT."
- **P4 (bottom, wide):** The Silvia drives off. Dai stands alone on the empty bypass.
  - CAPTION: "FORGET IT?"

## Page 14: School

- **P1 (top, wide):** Daytime classroom, summer. Open windows, cicadas. Dai at his desk in a black school uniform (collar open), sketching a Roadster's side profile in his notebook.
  - SFX: "MEEN MEEN"
- **P2 (middle-right):** A classmate bully snatches the notebook, another laughs.
  - BULLY: "HAYAMA'S DRAWING CARS? YOU CAN'T EVEN AFFORD A BIKE!"
- **P3 (middle-left):** Dai grabs the bully's wrist hard, a dangerous look. The bully flinches.
- **P4 (bottom):** Teacher's voice from off-panel; Dai lets go, takes the notebook back.
  - TEACHER (off-panel): "HAYAMA!"

## Page 15: It was the driver

- **P1 (top, wide):** A convenience store magazine rack. Dai reads a car magazine open to a page with a photo of a red Eunos Roadster.
  - CAPTION: "MAZDA EUNOS ROADSTER. 1.6 LITERS. ABOUT 120 HORSEPOWER."
- **P2 (middle-right):** Dai, frowning.
  - DAI: "120? THAT'S NOTHING."
- **P3 (middle-left):** Small inset memory: the white Silvia with its wing.
  - DAI (thought caption): "THAT SILVIA HAS WAY MORE. SO HOW..."
- **P4 (bottom, wide):** Dai closes the magazine, eyes sharp.
  - DAI: "IT WASN'T THE CAR."
  - DAI: "...IT WAS THE DRIVER."

## Page 16: Searching

Four-panel montage, each panel a different night.

- **P1 (top-right):** Dai on the overpass, empty road.
  - CAPTION: "NIGHT TWO."
- **P2 (top-left):** Same spot, rain, Dai soaked, hood up.
  - CAPTION: "NIGHT FIVE."
- **P3 (bottom-right):** Dai asking a group of street racers in a harbor parking lot; they laugh at him.
  - CAPTION: "NIGHT NINE."
  - RACER: "THE GHOST CAR? GO HOME, KID."
- **P4 (bottom-left):** Dai walking home alone along the harbor road, cranes behind.
  - CAPTION: "NIGHT ELEVEN."

## Page 17: The gas station

- **P1 (top, wide):** Dai walking on the harbor road at night. Ahead, a small 24-hour gas station glows under its canopy.
- **P2 (middle, large):** At the pump: the red Roadster, pop-up headlights DOWN, parked, immaculate, black top up.
- **P3 (bottom):** Dai freezes mid-step, eyes wide.

## Page 18: Wait

- **P1 (top-right):** Dai breaks into a sprint.
- **P2 (top-left, wide):** Close on the Roadster's right-side driver window: a hand in a black leather driving glove with open knuckles passes cash to an older night attendant. The driver's face is hidden in shadow.
- **P3 (bottom):** Dai running, shouting, hand outstretched.
  - DAI: "WAIT!!"

## Page 19: Gone

- **P1 (top-right):** The pop-up headlights flip open.
  - SFX: "KA-CHAK"
- **P2 (top-left, wide):** The Roadster pulls out past Dai. Through the window only a dark silhouette of the driver.
  - SFX: "WAAAN"
- **P3 (bottom, wide):** Red taillights shrinking down the harbor road; Dai alone under the canopy lights.

## Page 20: Every Thursday

- **P1 (top-right):** The older ATTENDANT, cap and uniform, counting the cash.
  - ATTENDANT: "THAT ONE? FILLS UP HERE EVERY THURSDAY."
  - ATTENDANT: "ALWAYS CASH. NEVER SAYS A WORD."
- **P2 (top-left):** Dai's fist clenches. For the first time in the chapter, a small grin.
- **P3 (bottom, large):** Dai standing under the gas station lights, looking down the road where the car went, determined.
  - CAPTION: "THAT NIGHT, I FOUND SOMETHING I WANTED MORE THAN ANYTHING."
  - CAPTION (small, bottom corner): "CHAPTER 1: RED AFTERIMAGE. END."
