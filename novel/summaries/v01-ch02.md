# Previously: Volume 1, Chapter 2, "Second Term"

Friday 1 September 1995, first day of second term. Hotta-sensei hands the third-years their job sheets; Dai's is Sanko Brake's Shiomi plant: brake linings, 148,000 yen a month, a dormitory, start 1 April. In the corridor Hotta tells him he already phoned Mom, and that the exam is on the 19th. At the shoe lockers Dai gives Kawamata his pen back and, for the first time, calls him "Nobu". Walking home he watches a girl stall on the driving-school hill five times.

That evening Mom fries karaage from 812-yen chicken that Aya finds in the bin ("No sticker... Who died?"). Mom counts Sanko's virtues on the table like bills: same day every month, sick pay, home by six. Dai says he'll sit it. She puts the last, burnt piece in his bowl and writes SANKO on the calendar in red. Aya gives him one week. Dai saves the piece and eats it cold after Mom leaves: "It was the best one."

Mom stamps the application with the family hanko on Saturday, and Dai hands it in on Tuesday. Kubo: "You can say no to a job. You can't say no to a job nobody gave you." In Tuesday's rain Kubo tapes Dai's open shoe and tears the tape with his teeth. Wednesday's pay is 44,800 yen; at dawn Dai keeps his promise and teaches Sota the feet on a phone book, his English dictionary and a folded cushion.

Thursday night, at 2:05 on Friday morning, the red Roadster is back. Natsume "had a shift". Dai asks her to teach him and won't take his hand off her window "till you say something that isn't no". She asks when he turns eighteen. "Get a license. Then we'll see." Alone at the pump he says it out loud: 280,000 yen.
