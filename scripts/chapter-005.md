# Chapter 5: "Second Term"

**Status:** draft. 30 pages, slow pacing.
**Source:** light novel Volume 1, Chapter 2 ("Second Term"), `novel/volume-01/chapter-02.md`.
**Reading order:** right-to-left.
**Story job:** second term starts. Dai gets one job sheet, Sanko Brake Industries, and finds out Hotta-sensei already talked to his mother. Nobu gets his pen back and a "thanks". Dai stops at the driving-school fence for the first time. Mom fries karaage at full price, reads the sheet twice and writes SANKO on the calendar; Dai says he'll sit the test. Kubo says sit it. Payday, the 40,000 in the dictionary, and the "feet" lesson for Sota on a phone book. Thursday night the red Roadster comes back, and Dai keeps his hand on her window until she says something that isn't no: "Get a license. Then we'll see."
**Lettering:** empty balloons, lettered by `production/az letter`. "Akai Zanzō" is lettered AKAI ZANZO; "Sōseki" is lettered SOSEKI (the font has no macron).
**Rules this chapter:** September, still hot: summer school uniform (white short-sleeved shirt). No shoes on tatami. Nights are DRY, except Tuesday night (p18, warm sideways rain). Right-hand drive: Akane sits on the car's RIGHT, the fuel door is on the RIGHT rear fender, the gear lever is to her LEFT. In the Thursday scene the camera stays on the car's right (driver's) side: Dai on the image left looking right, Akane in the window on the image right; in side views the car's front points RIGHT. Akane: black fingerless gloves with the knuckles cut out, hair tied back low. Every car shot has its own angle (list at the end).

**Dates (from the novel):** Fri 1 Sep 1995 (ceremony, sheet, karaage), Sat 2 Sep (hanko), Sun 3 Sep shift (4 a.m. Mon: Kubo), Tue 5 Sep (application; rain at midnight), Wed 6 Sep (6 a.m. payday; pedals at 6:50; rent envelope; receipt at night), Thu 7 Sep shift to Fri 8 Sep 2:05 a.m. (Natsume).
