# Previously: Volume 1, Chapter 6, "Night Shift"

Tuesday 26 September 1995, 1:54 a.m. Behind the curtain Natsume is someone else: a soft, slow voice for Sota ("Breathe out like you're blowing them out." "How... many?" "Eleven."), nothing for Dai. A young doctor puts in the drip and draws blood; Sota says sorry when the first try fails. Sota's glasses fog white and he wears them anyway. "I missed it," he says, of the siren. Dai phones Aya with 10 yen; she tells him to do his laces up.

Mom arrives by taxi at 2:35 in her green supermarket vest, wipes Sota's glasses twice, and does the forms with Natsume. At the night window she pays the 10,000-yen deposit with her last notes, her 500s and 100s, and thirty 10-yen coins. Natsume walks past and sees the coins. Sota goes up to the children's ward.

At 5:02 Natsume buys a hot corn soup at the vending machine and talks to Dai at last: a home nebulizer might help ("Ask his doctor"), then "Your mother works two jobs. Your brother can't breathe. And you want that road." "You drive it." "Nobody waits up for me." On the bench at dawn Mom shares her onigiri, says "I wrote the paper," lets slip that his father played pachinko through every hospital visit, and thanks him. Dai walks home.

He visits that afternoon while Mom and Sota sleep. Wednesday Nobu brings gyoza ("This is just gyoza"). On the ward Sota has kept the secret and wants the cat named; Dai tells him the hospital is free too.

Thursday night Natsume fills up, asks after Sota, then checks her tires by the air pump while Kubo talks to her for six minutes. Dai, busy with a fish truck, can't hear. Kubo: "Sunday. Six a.m. Tanabe's. She says bring gloves."
