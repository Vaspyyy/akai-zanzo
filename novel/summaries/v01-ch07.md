# Previously: Volume 1, Chapter 7, "Clutch"

Friday 29 September 1995, 2:15 a.m. Kubo admits he told Natsume where Dai goes on Sundays, and about Pier 3: "You asked me where it was. Then you never asked me anything about it again. Boys who don't go, ask." The cut is a pink line now.

Saturday after half-day school Dai buys white cotton gloves for 98 yen (100 with tax), so as not to turn up in Fumie's. Sota is home. Mom has the hospital envelope and won't say the amount: "It's paid." Sota asked her on the bus if the hospital was free, then how much the bus was. Sota does his new inhaler, Dai counting, and gives him four names for the cat: you have to ask it.

Sunday at 6:00 the red Roadster comes into Tanabe's. Fumie: "Two years, girl." She takes the cigarette out of her mouth, once, and calls her Akane-chan. In a back lot Fumie has walled in with flattened cars, Akane makes Dai set the seat, the mirrors and his hands, and asks what her feet did on the bypass. He knows. What her left foot did pulling off Kubo's forecourt, he doesn't.

Then he stalls it eleven times while Fumie counts from the forklift (she bet herself nine): a creeping foot, third gear, a panicked brake, too many revs, her handbrake, second at walking pace, shaking legs. "Nobody says anything about eleven." With his eyes shut he finds the bite by ear and through the seat. The twelfth pulls away; he does one lap in second without breathing.

Her rules: here only, never the bypass, "Lie to me once and we're done." He promises. She puts her seat and mirrors back exactly and leaves. Fumie: "That girl hasn't set foot in my yard since they brought in the..." "Get back to work."

## State at end
- About 6:45 a.m. Sunday 1 October, at Tanabe's. The rest of his third yard Sunday, then the Sunday shift; payday Wednesday 4 October.
- Dai has 42,887 yen (42,400 in the dictionary, 487 in pocket); 237,113 to the license. Owes Fumie 7,000 by 31 October; three of seven Sundays worked after today.
- He knows her first name is Akane. Lessons every Sunday at 6:00 in the back lot. The promise is given.
- Sota is home with a daily inhaler. Mom paid the bill; Dai doesn't know how much.
- The cut is uncovered and healing.

- The back lot: about 50 by 25 m, crushed shell; Corolla stacks at the near end, a new wall of Crowns at the far end, older stacks on the left, the back fence on the right; the blue Roadster's soft top shows through a gap in the Corolla stacks.
