# Chapter 4: "Practicing"

**Status:** draft. 30 pages, slow pacing.
**Source:** light novel Volume 1, Chapter 1 ("Practicing"), `novel/volume-01/chapter-01.md`.
**Reading order:** right-to-left.
**Story job:** the week after the lap. Dai practices on air, teaches Sota the gear pattern, meets Nobu Kawamata at remedial English, gets dusted out of a bookstore, works out on a receipt that half his pay buys a license by February, gets the "why" of the blip from Kubo, and waits all Thursday night for a car that doesn't come. At dawn he sees the newer guardrail and the scrubbed scrape under the overpass. Akane does not appear.
**Lettering:** empty balloons, lettered by `production/az letter`. "Akai Zanzō" is lettered AKAI ZANZO (the font has no macron).
**Rules this chapter:** Dai shifts with his LEFT hand (right-hand-drive car). Heel-and-toe is a downshift move. Nights are DRY and clear. August: summer school uniform (white short-sleeved shirt), not the gakuran. No shoes on tatami.

**Dates (PROVISIONAL, from the novel):** Fri 25 Aug 1995 (dawn to evening), Sun 27 Aug night, Thu 31 Aug night to Fri 1 Sep dawn.
