# Chapter 2: "Thursday"

**Status:** draft. 29 pages, slow pacing (creator wants 25-30 pages per chapter).
**Reading order:** right-to-left. "P1 (top-right)" = first panel.
**Story job:** show Dai's softer side (Sota), give him a real reason to take a job (the gas station), make the wait for Thursday hurt, then reveal Akane: calm, sharp, not impressed. End on the irony: his mother finally hears "I got a job," for the wrong reason.
**Driver rule:** Akane is hidden until page 20. On page 20 she is fully revealed.

---

- **P1 (top, wide):** The danchi block in morning light, laundry on balconies, a crow on a power line.
  - CAPTION: "THE NEXT MORNING."
  - SFX (small): "CAW"
- **P2 (bottom, wide):** Inside, tatami room. Dai asleep on a futon still in yesterday's T-shirt, one arm over his eyes. Morning light through the window. Beside him, Sota's futon, empty, sheets thrown back.

## Page 2: Sota
