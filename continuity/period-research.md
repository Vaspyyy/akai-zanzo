# Period research notes (1995 Japan): to verify before final scripting

- **Alcohol:** legal age 20. Street vending machines sold canned beer in 1995 (common until age-verification machines arrived around 2000).
- **Cannabis:** removed from the story; Japanese law and stigma make it far heavier than in Europe.
- **Licenses:** regular car license from 18; most people go through a driving school (~¥250,000-300,000). Verify 1995 prices.
- **Wages:** convenience-store part-time work ~¥650-750/hour in a regional city (verify).
- **Facilities:** 児童自立支援施設 (until 1998 called 教護院, kyōgoin). Use the 1995 term in dialogue if named.
- **Street-racing context:** mid-90s city racing (e.g. Osaka loop racers, Tokyo Wangan) drew heavy police crackdowns; arrests and confiscations were real consequences.
- **Tech:** pagers (pocket bell) were huge among teens in 1995; no mobile internet. Payphones and phone cards everywhere.
