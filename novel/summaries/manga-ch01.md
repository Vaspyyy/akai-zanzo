# Previously: manga chapter 1, "Red Afterimage"

Shiomi City, August 1995. Late at night in the family's cramped danchi apartment, Michiko Hayama tells her son Daisuke ("Dai"), 17, that the convenience store called: he skipped his shift again. His sister Aya, counting coins, points out that the "idiot" manager pays 650 yen an hour. Michiko lists what it takes to keep them afloat (Sota's asthma medicine went up, Aya gives half her pay, Michiko works two jobs) and tells him to face reality and keep a job. Dai walks out and slams the door.

He buys a canned beer from a vending machine and walks. Reality, he thinks, is a guy nobody remembers: the kid from the facility, four years in "the place they send kids nobody can handle." On the pedestrian overpass above the Shiomi Bypass he thinks that nobody has ever looked at him and thought "wow."

At 2:14 a.m. two pop-up headlights flip open in the dark. A red Eunos Roadster, with Goto's white Silvia S13 tailgating it, takes the inside line through the long left-hand curve under the overpass and leaves the Silvia behind. Dai drops his beer. He runs down to Goto, who tells him the harbor crowd calls the car the "Akai Zanzō", the red afterimage: you chase it and only ever catch the afterimage. "Some rich guy's toy. Forget it."

He can't. At school he sketches the car and grabs a bully's wrist. A magazine says it has only about 120 horsepower, so it wasn't the car, it was the driver. After eleven nights of searching he spots the Roadster at a 24-hour harbor gas station, but it leaves before he reaches it. The old attendant: "Fills up here every Thursday. Always cash. Never says a word."

## State at end
- Dai has a lead: the harbor station, Thursdays.
- The fight with Michiko is unresolved; the convenience-store job is effectively gone.
- Goto has met "the bridge kid" once and is bitter about losing.
- Dai has found "something I wanted more than anything."
